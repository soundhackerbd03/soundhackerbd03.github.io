( About )

Sound Hacker is an audio production house in the heart of Dhaka. Built on years of FM radio and a rock band’s heart, we compose, record, design and mix sound for artists, brands and screen — from a ten-second jingle to a full album.

Sound Hacker began in 2018 as two friends with one obsession: composer, producer and sound engineer Shariful Islam Ghuddy and Md Rizvi Rahman. Today their Kakrail studio brings composition, recording, sound design, mixing and mastering under one roof.

Our roots are on air and on stage. Before Sound Hacker, Ghuddy spent years shaping sound for FM radio — Radio Bhumi 92.8 from 2012, Colours FM 101.6 from 2015 — while fronting the patriotic rock band Ghuddy. That mix of broadcast precision and a musician’s instinct is still how we work: every second has to earn its place, and every track has to make you feel something.

Today we make songs and albums, TV-drama and film scores, jingles and ad campaigns, NGO projects, dubbing and podcasts. One promise stays the same: your sound, hacked to perfection.
